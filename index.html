01 / Featured project

Tools for robots that learn.

Connecting robot definitions, simulation, data, and learning in one training workflow.

Embody · Prototype

An Isaac-native
policy training toolkit.

Embody helps define robots, scenes, tasks, observations, and actions—then connects data collection, training, policy artifacts, and evaluation.

I’m building a coherent path from a robotics task to a policy you can inspect and evaluate. Deployed policies still need application-specific calibration, observations, and control loops.

Inside the toolkit

Project-owned tasks and dependency environments keep each training project self-contained. Typed references resolve tasks and robots; versioned artifacts track datasets, checkpoints, policies, and evaluations. Dry, fast, and vision profiles support resource checks, Isaac execution without camera rendering, and camera-based review.

Embody is an evolving prototype. APIs, storage layouts, policy interfaces, and CLI commands may change as the design develops.

02 / Sim-to-real research

Robotics Simulation & Policy Research Assistant
Dr. Jiang’s Lab · SJSU · July 2026–present

Ongoing research

Contact-rich manipulation

I engineer a high-fidelity digital twin of the Franka Research 3 and Realhand L6 in NVIDIA Isaac Sim, integrating kinematics, actuation, and contact models for manipulation benchmarks.

Using Isaac Lab and PyTorch, I developed a simulation pipeline for parallel training across 128 environments. I also process synchronized teleoperation data to fit friction, compliance, and actuation delay, and support reinforcement learning with imitation-learned priors and calibrated domain randomization.
