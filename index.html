Research

Robotic Hip Exoskeleton for Gait Abnormalities

Dr. Jiang's Robotics Rehabilitation Lab · San Jose State University

The purpose of this project was to enhance a robotic hip exoskeleton's gait support through policy training using IMU and EMG sensor data. I designed and 3D-printed a custom knee holster for the sensors, and my team conducted data collection sessions across various participants to gather high-quality datasets for robot policy training. Additionally, I leveraged NVIDIA Isaac Sim to develop a digital twin by 3D-scanning the robot into SolidWorks, rigging the design with its mechanical functionalities, and importing it into Isaac Sim. Although this work was paused due to project timeline constraints, it remains a promising direction that I intend to explore further.
